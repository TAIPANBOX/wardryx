# Wardryx 2.8.0

2.8.0 gives every hold an end. Until now a hold lived until a person decided it, so on a deployment where only a passkey decides, a hold that a test or a departed agent left behind stayed in the queue and under the pending cap for good. A hold now closes in exactly three ways: a person decides it, wardryx closes it as `expired` once it has waited `WARDRYX_HOLD_TTL`, or the held agent withdraws it (CLAUDE.md invariant 52). An admin key cancels nothing: a person who does not want a call to go ahead denies it.

It is a minor release. No frozen name is removed or renamed, no decision on a call changes, and `/v1/approvals/{id}/decide` still takes only `grant` and `deny`.

Tag: `v2.8.0`. The image is `ghcr.io/taipanbox/wardryx:v2.8.0`. There is no moving tag (no `latest`, no `main`).

## Upgrade

- **Holds already waiting longer than 24 hours close at the first start.** The lifetime check runs once at start and once a minute. Every pending hold whose `requested_at` is at least `WARDRYX_HOLD_TTL` ago (24 hours unless set) is closed as `expired` by that first check, with one `approval_expired` event each. To keep such holds open a little longer, set `WARDRYX_HOLD_TTL` up to `168h` before the upgrade, and decide them in that time.
- **No schema step.** `expired` and `withdrawn` are values of the existing `decision` column, and closing a hold deletes its sealed preview in the same statement, as a decision does.
- **Consoles and readers.** `GET /v1/approvals` and `wardryx approvals` list the closed rows with `pending` false, `decision` `expired` or `withdrawn`, and `approver_kind` `system` or `agent`. A reader that took every decision other than `grant` for a refusal by a person reads these wrongly; genaryx reads them as what they are from its invariant 49 (cb7f779) on. A console that does not know them still decides every pending hold as before.
- **Several instances.** Move every instance to 2.8.0 together. An instance on 2.7.0 closes nothing on a timer and has no withdraw route (`404`), so until every instance runs 2.8.0 a withdraw may land on one that cannot answer it. Of several 2.8.0 instances on one database, each hold is closed, and recorded, by one of them.
- **`WARDRYX_HOLD_TTL`** is a Go duration from `1h` to `168h`; anything else refuses to start. There is no value that turns it off.

## Rollback

Run the 2.7.0 image again. Rows already closed as `expired` or `withdrawn` stay closed: 2.7.0 counts and decides only rows with no decision, lists the closed ones with their `decision` and an empty `approver_kind`, and answers a decide on one as it answers any decided hold. Holds go back to living until a person decides them.

## Added

- **The hold lifetime.** `WARDRYX_HOLD_TTL`, unset 24 hours. A pending hold that has waited it gets `decision` `expired`, `decided_by` `wardryx:hold-ttl` and `decided_at`, and no token. Its place under `WARDRYX_PENDING_PER_AGENT` is free again and the agent's next call is a new hold. On Postgres the rows are taken `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` and updated only while still undecided, in batches of 500, so a hold a person decided first is left alone.
- **`POST /v1/approvals/{id}/withdraw`.** The held agent, through the enforcement point that speaks for it, closes its own pending hold with `{"agent_id": ..., "run_id": ...}`: `decision` `withdrawn`, `decided_by` `agent:` and the agent id, no token. With `WARDRYX_AGENT_TOKEN_ISSUER` set the request also carries `agent_token` and `agent_dpop_proof`, checked as on `/v1/decide` before the hold is read. An admin or cross-org admin key is `403`; a hold of another agent, run or org is the same `404` as an unknown id; a hold already closed is `409`.
- **Two event types under source `wardryx`**, listed in agent-passport SPEC 6.2: `approval_expired` (medium; `approval_id`, `waited_seconds`, `ttl_seconds`, `decided_by`, `org`) and `approval_withdrawn` (info; `approval_id`, `decided_by`, `key_fp`, `waited_seconds`, `org`). Neither is `approval_denied`.

## Limits

- The cutoff is the instance's clock against `requested_at`, which the instance that made the hold wrote, so two instances with clocks apart close a hold that far apart.
- A check that cannot reach the store is logged once per outage and closes nothing until it can. The event of a closure is written fail-open, as a grant's is; the row is the durable record.
- Without `WARDRYX_AGENT_TOKEN_ISSUER`, the enforcement point's key is believed about which agent withdraws, as it is on `/v1/decide`.
- The agent learns that its hold closed only by polling `GET /v1/approvals` or by asking again.
