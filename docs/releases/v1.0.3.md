# Wardryx 1.0.3

One fix for a policy store that stops answering, found on the 2026-09-17
appliance proving run, and nothing on the decision path moves.

## The defect (wardryx#62)

With the Postgres policy store stopped, `GET /healthz` still answered 200, a
`PUT /v1/policies/{id}` had no answer after eight seconds, and the log said
nothing about the store until the client gave up. A launcher or an operator
watching the health route saw a healthy plane that could not take a policy
write.

## The fix (wardryx#63)

- **Readiness reads the store; liveness still does not.** A new
  `GET /readyz`, unauthenticated like `/healthz`, pings the store under a
  three-second deadline and answers `200 {"store":"ok"}` or
  `503 {"store":"unreachable"}`. `/healthz` and `POST /v1/decide` are untouched.
- **A policy write is bounded.** `PUT` and `DELETE /v1/policies/{id}` share one
  three-second deadline over every store call and answer 503 with a sentence
  this plane composed, never the driver's error text (invariant 10).
- **The outage is said once.** The log carries one line when the store becomes
  unreachable and one when it is back, however many requests met it.

Invariant 20 holds the rule; `features/store-outage.feature` binds fifteen
scenarios to tests, each red first against 1.0.2; seven mutants were planted
and caught; the Postgres half ran against a real `postgres:16` in CI.

## Also in this release

- The 2026-09-17 appliance run recorded in `VALIDATION.md` (#64).
- agent-stack-go v1.0.2 (#65); the event, passport and chain packages this
  plane imports are unchanged between v1.0.1 and v1.0.2.

## Compatibility

Additive: `/readyz` is a new route, not frozen in `compat/1.0.json`; nothing on
the wire, in the decision request or response, in the policy fields or in the
event types moves. A 1.0.x deployment moves to 1.0.3 by moving the image tag;
a launcher that wants the readiness probe points it at `/readyz`.

## Limits

Reads under `/v1/policies`, `/v1/approvals` and `/v1/status`, the approval
paths on `/v1/decide`, the unanswered-approval sweep and `OpenPostgres` at
startup are not bounded by the new deadline. A write the store applied after
the deadline is restored on the next start; until then the store and the live
set can differ. The appliance itself was not re-run with this build.
